I'm Kalina, a Stockholm-based creative designer drawn to visual storytelling, thoughtful design and ideas that make people feel something.

My background in computer and systems sciences, with a focus on digital media, lets me bring both creative thinking and technical curiosity to my work. I've worked with web design, content creation, social media, filming and editing.

I'm especially interested in editorial design, art direction and creative production — where concept, image and typography come together.
