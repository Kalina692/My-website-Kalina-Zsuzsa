About Me

My name is Kalina Zsuzsa, I'm 21 years old and live in Stockholm. I'm currently in my third year of the Bachelor's program in Computer and Systems Sciences with a specialization in Digital Media at Stockholm University, and I will be graduating soon.

I explore everything from programming and app development to 3D modeling, digital storytelling, and game development. I'm driven by creating digital experiences that engage, move, and innovate. From coding in the classroom to experimenting with sound, visuals, and interactive projects, I constantly bridge the gap between technology and creativity.

Through collaborations, prototypes, and immersive projects, I've developed skills in 3D modeling, animation, VR, XR, and transmedia storytelling. My goal is to craft digital experiences that are both meaningful and memorable, combining technical expertise with artistic sensibility.

The future is digital, and I aim to be at the forefront — not just as a student, but as a creative force who thinks big, builds smart, and dreams beautifully.
